Straight answers

Know exactly what you are building.

The Private Label Program is for firms that want a branded platform and the flexibility to create a distinct commercial offer for their clients.

Ask a Private Label question →
Is Private Label the same as white label?

Yes. We use “Private Label” for the service: your clients use the system on your custom domain with your brand identity while MyDigitalInvoice powers the underlying platform.

How do I receive the 50% setup discount?

Use the code WHITE LABLE exactly as written. It reduces the regular one-time price from Rs. 140,000 to Rs. 70,000.

What does the 30% monthly discount cover?

It applies to the standard monthly package brackets: Basic becomes Rs. 350, Starter Rs. 700, Standard Rs. 1,400 and Unlimited Rs. 2,100, before 5% service tax.

Can my firm create and sell custom packages?

Yes. Your firm can design client-facing packages and choose its own commercial pricing. Any revenue outcome depends on your own pricing, sales and client relationships.

Are FBR and PRA included as one service?

No. FBR Digital Invoicing and PRA eIMS Integration remain separate authority services. Activation and billing may apply separately depending on the service you choose to offer.
