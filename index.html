Questions, answered

Know what you are activating before you begin.

Clear answers on FBR submission, wallet billing, PRA integration, and onboarding.

Still unsure?Talk to a local integration specialist.Ask on WhatsApp →
What does the FBR digital invoicing workflow require?

Sales tax-registered businesses create structured digital invoices containing the required buyer, item, HS, tax, and transaction details. The invoice is transmitted through the connected workflow and receives an authority response, including the IRN and QR record where applicable.

How does submission from MyDigitalInvoice work?

Create or import the invoice in your portal, validate the transaction data, and submit it through the connected FBR workflow. The response and resulting invoice record remain linked to the original transaction.

How does wallet-based pricing work?

Your monthly invoice count selects the Basic, Starter, Standard, or Unlimited bracket. We add 5% service tax and deduct the bill from wallet credits. A regular PKR 5,000 top-up adds PKR 7,000 credits, while optional annual advance funding receives a 40% bonus. Integration is included with wallet funding.

When do integration charges apply?

The one-time integration charge (PKR 4,500 for business owners, PKR 2,500 per client company for accountants) applies when a client chooses monthly billing without making the PKR 5,000 wallet top-up. If the wallet is funded, integration is included and the PKR 5,000 top-up adds PKR 7,000 credits for monthly bills.

How long does onboarding take?

About 15–20 minutes on Google Meet. Our team sets up your company and completes the FBR integration with you on the call.

Do you need my FBR IRIS login?

Yes. During onboarding we ask for your FBR IRIS login and the OTP FBR sends you, because it is required to complete the Digital Invoicing integration. We use it only for the integration, and you can change your IRIS password afterwards.

Do you support PRA eIMS Integration?

Yes. PRA eIMS Integration is available as a separate service for Punjab service businesses. It uses the same invoice-volume brackets and rates as FBR Digital Invoicing, but each authority service is activated and billed separately.

Can an accountant manage multiple client companies?

Yes. The accountant workflow is designed for multi-client access, client-specific status, bulk processing, reports, and audit history from one operating workspace.

Can my firm launch MyDigitalInvoice under its own brand?

Yes. The Private Label Program gives firms a client-facing platform on a custom domain with custom branding and the ability to create their own packages. The one-time setup is Rs. 140,000, plus standard monthly package rates.
