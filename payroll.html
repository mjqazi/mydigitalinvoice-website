Questions

Payroll FAQ

Which statutory contributions does Payroll cover?

EOBI, provincial professional tax, provincial social security (IESSI and KPESSI), the Workers' Welfare Fund (WWF) and the Workers' Children Education Cess.

Where do I get the Payroll app?

MDI Payroll is available for Android on Google Play. Sign in with your My Digital Invoice account.

Does payroll connect to my accounts?

Yes. Payroll is part of the MDI platform, and payroll and company expenses post to your general ledger so your chart of accounts stays current without double entry.

Who is Payroll for?

Any business with salaried staff, and accountants who run payroll for their clients.
