When the internet drops

Keep selling. The till catches up on its own.

You don't need to do anything differently during an outage. The customer still gets a real invoice, and the sale is filed under the time it actually happened.

  1. Ring the sale normally

    The till already holds reserved invoice numbers, so it can issue a real document without waiting.

  2. An offline invoice prints

    It carries your sale number and every line of the sale, clearly marked as issued offline.

  3. The sale waits in a queue

    It is stored with the exact time it was rung, so it files under the right day.

  4. It files itself

    When the connection returns the sale files with the same number, and the full receipt can be reprinted.

End-of-day check: open the sales and filing history to see every paid sale as filed, waiting to be filed or needing attention. A sale that needs a decision waits in a queue for a person — it is never re-rung or filed twice.

Questions

POS FAQ

Do I need a separate POS login?

No. The till uses your My Digital Invoice account — the same email and password as the portal. If you are already signed in, open POS from the dashboard.

What happens if the internet goes down?

Keep selling. The till prints an invoice marked as issued offline, queues the sale with its exact time and files it automatically when the connection returns — with the same sale number.

How do I know every sale was filed?

Open the sales and filing history at the end of a shift. Each paid sale shows whether it is filed, waiting to be filed or needs attention.

How is POS billed?

POS uses the same integration charges, invoice-volume brackets and monthly rates as FBR Digital Invoicing, and is billed as a separate service. See the pricing table.
