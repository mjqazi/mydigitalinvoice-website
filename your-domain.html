Straight answers

Know exactly what you are building.

Your Domain is for firms that want a branded platform and the flexibility to create a distinct commercial offer for their clients.

Ask a Private Label question →
Is Your Domain the same as white label?

Yes. Your Domain is our white-label service (previously called Private Label): your clients use the system on your custom domain with your brand identity while MyDigitalInvoice powers the underlying platform.

How much does Your Domain setup cost?

The one-time Your Domain setup price is Rs. 140,000. Under the current launch offer it is available at up to 50% off, as low as Rs. 70,000.

What monthly fees apply?

The standard monthly package rates are Basic Rs. 500, Starter Rs. 1,000, Standard Rs. 2,000 and Unlimited Rs. 3,000, before 5% service tax.

Can my firm create and sell custom packages?

Yes. Your firm can design client-facing packages and choose its own commercial pricing. Any revenue outcome depends on your own pricing, sales and client relationships.

Are FBR and PRA included as one service?

No. FBR Digital Invoicing and PRA eIMS Integration remain separate authority services. Activation and billing may apply separately depending on the service you choose to offer.
