Questions

PRA Integration FAQ

What is PRA Integration?

PRA Integration connects your invoicing workflow with PRA eIMS electronic invoicing workflows for Punjab sales tax on services.

Who is PRA Integration for?

It is designed for businesses providing taxable services in Punjab that need to use PRA electronic invoicing workflows. Your exact registration and compliance requirements should be confirmed against your PRA profile or with your tax adviser.

Are the packages the same as FBR Digital Invoicing?

Yes. PRA Integration is priced separately using the same Basic, Starter, Standard and Unlimited invoice brackets and monthly rates shown above. Price parity does not mean the services are bundled.

Does PRA Integration include FBR Digital Invoicing?

No. PRA Integration and FBR Digital Invoicing are separate services. Paying for one does not include the other.

How are PRA Integration charges paid?

PRA Integration uses the same published wallet funding model. Monthly PRA usage plus 5% service tax on MyDigitalInvoice's charge is deducted from wallet credits for the PRA service. Regular PKR 5,000 top-ups receive PKR 7,000 credits, while annual advance funding receives 50% bonus credits. Customers using both PRA and FBR services should confirm their account setup with our team.

MyDigitalInvoice is an independent software provider. References to PRA and PRA eIMS identify the authority and system supported; they do not imply sponsorship or endorsement by the Punjab Revenue Authority.
