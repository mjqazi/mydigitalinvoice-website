Upload thousands of invoices from one Excel file.

For wholesalers, distributors and high-volume businesses: drop an Excel file, approve the preview and come back to IRNs. Validation happens before anything is submitted, and an interrupted run picks up exactly where it stopped.

  • Excel in, IRNs out. Headers are parsed and every row is checked against NTN, HS code, tax rate and sale type before submission.

Who uses bulk upload? Wholesalers and distributors with hundreds of supply orders a day, retail chains consolidating end-of-day sales, manufacturers pushing dispatch invoices, and accounting firms clearing client backlogs.

How a bulk batch flows

  1. Drop your Excel

    Any standard invoice export — from your ERP, POS or a hand-kept sheet. .xls and .xlsx are both accepted.

  2. Validation runs first

    Every row is checked for NTN/CNIC, HS code, sale type, tax rate, quantity and unit. Bad rows are flagged with the exact error.

  3. You approve the preview

    See every row and its status, fix issues inline or re-upload. Nothing is submitted until you approve it.

  4. Submission runs

    Validated invoices are submitted one by one and each IRN and QR is stored. Progress is checkpointed continuously.

  5. You get a final report

    Submitted and failed counts with reasons. Failed rows can be fixed and re-run without touching the file again.

Digital Invoicing FAQ

Who needs FBR digital invoicing?

Sales tax-registered businesses — manufacturers, importers, wholesalers, distributors, retailers and service providers — are required to issue digital invoices with an IRN and QR code. Not sure it applies to you? Take the 60-second eligibility check.

How is Digital Invoicing billed?

By monthly invoice volume, from wallet credits: Basic Rs 500 (0–5 invoices), Starter Rs 1,000 (6–20), Standard Rs 2,000 (21–200) or Unlimited Rs 3,000 (201+), each plus 5% service tax. Every regular Rs 5,000 top-up adds Rs 7,000 in credits. See the pricing table.

Is Bulk Upload included?

Bulk Upload is an optional add-on at Rs 2,000 + 5% service tax a month, with no invoice cap. It stacks on top of any usage bracket and is deducted from wallet credits.

What happens if a bulk upload is interrupted?

Batches are checkpointed continuously. If the connection drops or a server restarts, the run resumes at the exact invoice that was pending — no duplicates and no lost work.

Does Digital Invoicing include PRA?

No. FBR Digital Invoicing and PRA eIMS are separate services, billed separately at the same monthly brackets.
